# 1. Introduction to Bioinformatics
---

## 1.1 What is Bioinformatics?

Bioinformatics is the application of biology, computer science, statistics, and data analysis to understand and analyze biological data.

A simple way to remember it:

**Biological Data + Computing + Statistics = Bioinformatics**


## 1.2 Why is Bioinformatics Important?

Modern biological research produces huge amounts of data. Major sub-fields include:

- Genomics – analysis of complete genomes.

- Transcriptomics – analysis of RNA and gene expression.

- Infectious Disease Genomics – study of pathogens using genomic data.

- AMR Bioinformatics – identification and analysis of antimicrobial resistance genes.

- Variant Analysis – identification of SNPs, insertions, deletions, etc.

- Metagenomics – analysis of genetic material from microbial communities.


## 1.3 Why Linux?

Linux is the operating system most widely used for scientific computing and bioinformatics. Most bioinformatics tools run through the Linux command line, e.g. `FastQC`, `MultiQC`, `Salmon`, `STAR`, `HISAT2`, `DESeq2`, `SAMtools`, `BWA`, `SPAdes`, `AMRFinderPlus`.

**Learning Linux is one of the most important first steps in bioinformatics.**

> **Note:** When you open a terminal you will see a prompt such as username@computer:\~$ — the $ means the terminal is ready for a command.
